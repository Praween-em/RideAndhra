Drive with Ride Andhra

Connect with riders looking for transportation in your area. Ride Andhra is an information platform that helps riders find drivers and get estimated fare information for their journeys.

Why Connect with Ride Andhra?

No Platform Commission

Ride Andhra does not charge commission on your rides. You negotiate fares directly with riders.

Flexible Working Hours

Choose when you want to drive. There are no minimum hours or shifts required.

Local Rides, Local Support

Connect with riders in your local area. Support available for app-related issues.

Direct Rider Connections

Connect directly with riders and agree on terms that work for both parties.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Valid driving license - Must be valid for the vehicle type you operate
  • Vehicle documents - Registration, insurance, and any required permits
  • Smartphone with internet - Required to use the Ride Andhra Driver App

Important Information for Driver Partners

Payment and Financial Arrangements: Ride Andhra is an information-only platform that provides estimated fares and connects riders with drivers. We do not process payments, collect fares, or handle any financial transactions between riders and drivers.

Direct Negotiation: All fare agreements, payments, and financial arrangements are made directly between you (the driver) and the rider, outside of the Ride Andhra platform.

No Employment Relationship: Ride Andhra does not employ drivers, provide vehicles, or guarantee any minimum earnings. You operate as an independent service provider.

Important Note: Ride Andhra does not pay money to drivers. Any payments for rides occur directly between riders and drivers. Ride Andhra's role is limited to providing information and connectivity services.
