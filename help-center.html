Popular Questions

How do I book a Bike Taxi?
Simply open the Ride Andhra app, enter your destination, select the 'Bike' option, and tap 'Book Ride'. A partner will be assigned to you shortly.
What is the 0% commission model for drivers?
Unlike other apps, we don't take a percentage of your fare. Drivers pay a small daily subscription of ₹24 and keep 100% of the ride fare.
How are parcel delivery charges calculated?
Charges are based on the distance between the pickup and drop-off points, as well as the size/weight category of the item.
Is my ride insured?
Yes, we provide insurance coverage support for all active rides booked through the Ride Andhra platform for both riders and drivers.
