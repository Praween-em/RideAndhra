Important Information About Our Service

Ride Andhra operates as an information and connectivity platform only. Please note:

  • We do not provide transportation services, own vehicles, or employ drivers
  • All fare values shown are estimates only and may vary
  • We do not process payments, collect fares, or maintain wallets
  • Any ride agreement, payment, or dispute is strictly between rider and driver
  • Drivers are independent third parties and we do not supervise or guarantee their behavior
