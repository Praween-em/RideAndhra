Privacy Policy

Ride Andhra ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Ride Andhra mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and profile details provided during registration.
  • Location Data: Real-time and background location to enable ride booking, driver matching, navigation, and safety features.
  • Ride Information: Pickup and drop locations, trip history, timestamps, and fare details.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, app version, and crash logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide ride booking and transportation services
  • To connect riders with nearby drivers
  • To calculate fares and process payments
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To ensure safety, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements

3. Location Permissions

Ride Andhra collects location data even when the app is running in the background to enable live ride tracking, navigation, and safety features. Location data is used only for ride-related functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Drivers or riders involved in a trip
  • Payment gateways for transaction processing
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Children’s Privacy

Ride Andhra is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. User Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
