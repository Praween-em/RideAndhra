Privacy Policy
How we handle your data and protect your privacy

Introduction — Ride Andhra ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of our Driver Partners ("Captains"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Ride Andhra Captain mobile application.

1. Information Collection

  • Personal Information: Full name, phone number, email address, profile photo, and government-issued identification (Aadhaar, Driving License, PAN).
  • Vehicle Information: Registration Certificate (RC), insurance documents, permit details, and vehicle model.
  • Location Data (CRITICAL): Precise GPS location when the app is in foreground or background (while you are marked "Online"). This is essential to receive ride requests, enable rider tracking, and calculate accurate fare & distance.
  • Transaction Data: Ride history, fare earned, platform fees, cancellations, and ratings.

2. Use of Information

We use collected data to:

  • Verify your identity and driving eligibility.
  • Match you with ride requests efficiently.
  • Enable real-time navigation and safety features (e.g., share live location with riders).
  • Process payouts and compute GST settlement where applicable.
  • Analyze service quality, monitor driver performance, and improve app reliability.

3. Data Sharing

  • With Riders: During an active trip, riders can see your name, photo, vehicle model, license plate, and live location.
  • With Authorities: We may share information with law enforcement, transport authorities, or courts to comply with legal obligations in Andhra Pradesh / India.
  • Service Providers: Trusted partners (Razorpay for payouts, Google Maps for navigation, cloud hosting) have limited access under confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption and access controls. Your data is retained while your Captain account is active, and for longer periods as required by tax, audit, or legal mandates (e.g., financial records up to 7 years). You may request deletion, but some data may be kept for fraud prevention or compliance.

5. Your Rights

You can review and edit your profile information inside the app. For data access, rectification or account deletion requests, contact help.rideandhra@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days as per applicable law.

📍 Background location consent: By toggling "Online" status, you explicitly agree that Ride Andhra will collect real-time location data even when the app is in the background. This is mandatory for ride allocation and rider safety. You may revoke location permissions in device settings, but this will prevent you from receiving trip requests.
