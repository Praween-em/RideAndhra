Captain agreement – rights, duties, and operational rules

1. The Agreement

By registering as a Captain on Ride Andhra, you agree to these Terms. You represent that you are at least 18 years old and possess a valid driving license (commercial or adapted as per Motor Vehicles Act, 1988).

2. Driver Obligations

  • Accuracy: Provide true and current documents (Driver’s License, RC, Insurance, Pollution under control). Any fraudulent documentation leads to immediate termination and possible legal proceedings.
  • Safety & maintenance: Keep the vehicle roadworthy, follow traffic rules, and ensure valid third-party insurance.
  • Professionalism: Maintain respectful behaviour, avoid cancellations after accepting rides, and aim for high ratings. Persistent low ratings may result in account review or suspension.

3. Financial Terms

  • Platform Fees: A transparent platform fee (plus applicable GST) is deducted per completed ride. The exact fee is shown before trip acceptance.
  • Subscription Plans: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly zero-commission plans are available – the subscription fee is non-refundable and offers benefits during the chosen period.
  • Payouts: Earnings are settled through Razorpay (or equivalent) to your linked bank account as per schedule (typically weekly, with T+3 settlement).
  • Tax Responsibility: You are solely responsible for individual income tax, GST registration (if applicable based on turnover), and any other statutory dues. Ride Andhra may issue tax invoices and deduct TDS where mandated.

4. Location Tracking & Consent

You acknowledge that enabling location services while "Online" is a condition for using the platform. Ride Andhra uses background location to assign nearby rides, monitor trip progress and prevent misuse. Disabling location during online mode will log you out automatically.

5. Code of Conduct & Prohibitions

  • No Off-app transactions: Soliciting or accepting rides outside the Ride Andhra app is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent deactivation and forfeiture of outstanding earnings.
  • Account integrity: Never share your Captain account with any other individual. Multi-usage leads to suspension.
  • Zero-tolerance policy: Harassment, discrimination, driving under influence, unsafe manoeuvres, or any illegal activity leads to immediate termination.
  • Manipulation: Fake trips, artificial distance inflation, or collusion with riders to evade fees will trigger legal action and recovery of damages.

6. Termination & Suspension

Ride Andhra may suspend or terminate access if you materially breach these Terms, pose a safety threat, engage in fraud, or receive multiple critical safety complaints. You may deactivate your account voluntarily via support. Upon termination, you must uninstall the app and cease use of all Captain services.
